Body measurements

A place under Account where you store your body measurements in cm, with a date on each log, so you see a history and a trend for each body part.

What changes and why

  • Now the app keeps only height and body weight. Weight has a history. Nothing else about your body is stored.
  • After this change, Account › Body and health has a new row, Measurements. It opens a page with all your body measurements.
  • Two kinds of measurement, as we agreed.
    • Circumferences (change with training) · neck, shoulders, chest, waist, hips, upper arm, forearm, thigh, calf. The arm and leg parts have a left and a right value. Each one has a history and a trend.
    • Lengths (do not change in an adult) · arm length, leg length, inseam. The page shows the latest value only.
  • Your old list fits in. Height stays where it is now.

How to measure

Rules for each measurement

  • Use a soft tape that does not stretch. Measure on the skin, not over clothes.
  • Measure in the morning, before you eat and before you train. Use the same conditions each time.
  • Stand straight and relaxed, with the feet together and the arms at the sides.
  • Keep the tape level with the floor. It must touch the skin all around, but not press into it.
  • Read the value at the end of a normal breath out. Read to 0.5 cm.
  • Measure two times. If the two values are more than 1 cm apart, measure a third time. Log the middle value.
  • Measure every 2 to 4 weeks. Changes of less than 1 cm from one log to the next are usually measurement noise.

The points and methods follow ISO 8559-1 (body dimensions for clothing) and the WHO method for the waist.

Circumferences

1Neck

Just below the Adam's apple (larynx).

  1. Look straight ahead. Keep the shoulders down.
  2. Put the tape around the neck. Let it go a little down at the front.
  3. Do not tighten the neck.

Tip · Do not swallow while you read the value.

2Shoulders

Around the widest part of the shoulders, over the round shoulder muscle.

  1. Stand with the arms relaxed at the sides.
  2. Put the tape around both shoulders and the upper back.
  3. Breathe out normally, then read.

Tip · Ask a helper. It is hard to keep the tape level at the back yourself.

3Chest

Around the fullest part of the chest, at nipple level, under the armpits.

  1. Lift the arms a little. Put the tape around the chest and over the shoulder blades.
  2. Lower the arms to the sides.
  3. Breathe out normally, then read.

Tip · Do not fill the lungs and do not tighten the chest.

4Waist

Halfway between the lowest rib and the top of the hip bone. For most people this is the narrowest part, a little above the navel.

  1. Stand with the feet together and the stomach relaxed.
  2. Put the tape around the waist, level with the floor.
  3. Breathe out normally, then read at the end of the breath.

Tip · Do not pull in the stomach. Use the same point each time.

5Hips

Around the widest part of the buttocks.

  1. Stand with the feet together.
  2. Put the tape around the hips and buttocks.
  3. Move the tape up and down to find the widest part. Read there.

Tip · Look at the side in a mirror to make sure the tape is level.

6Upper arm left and right

Halfway between the tip of the shoulder and the elbow.

  1. Let the arm hang relaxed. Do not tighten the muscle.
  2. Put the tape around the arm, at a right angle to it.
  3. Measure the left arm, then the right arm.

Tip · Measure relaxed each time, so the values agree.

7Forearm left and right

Around the widest part, just below the elbow.

  1. Let the arm hang. Turn the palm to the front. Open the hand.
  2. Move the tape down from the elbow to find the widest part.
  3. Measure the left arm, then the right arm.

Tip · Do not make a fist.

8Thigh left and right

Halfway between the groin crease and the top of the kneecap.

  1. Stand with the weight on both feet. Relax the leg.
  2. Put the tape around the thigh, level with the floor.
  3. Measure the left leg, then the right leg.

Tip · Measure the distance from the kneecap once. Then use the same distance each time.

9Calf left and right

Around the widest part of the calf.

  1. Stand with the weight on both feet.
  2. Move the tape up and down to find the widest part.
  3. Measure the left leg, then the right leg.

Tip · Do not stand on the toes.

Lengths

10

10Arm length

From the tip of the shoulder (the bony point at the top) to the wrist bone.

  1. Let the arm hang with a small bend at the elbow.
  2. Hold the tape on the tip of the shoulder.
  3. Go along the outside of the arm, over the elbow, to the wrist bone.

Tip · A helper must do this one.

11

11Leg length

From the waist at the side of the body down to the floor.

  1. Stand barefoot with the feet a little apart.
  2. Hold the tape at the side of the waist.
  3. Let it go straight down the outside of the leg to the floor.

Tip · Use the same waist point as for measurement 4.

12

12Inseam

From the crotch down to the floor, on the inside of the leg.

  1. Stand barefoot against a wall, with the feet 15 cm apart.
  2. Push a book up between the legs, level with the floor. Mark the wall at the top of the book.
  3. Measure from the mark to the floor.

Tip · The book method is more accurate than holding the tape in the crotch.

Done
  • One part per screen, in a fixed order. First the tape around the trunk from the top down (neck, shoulders, chest, waist, hips). Then the right arm, the left arm, the right leg and the left leg. You do not move the tape back and forth, and you do not search a long form.
  • 13 steps for circumference. Each arm and leg part is its own step, right side first.
  • The number keypad opens at once. The field starts with your last value, selected, so you type over it. Next on the keypad goes to the next step.
  • Fast buttons. − 0.5 and + 0.5 change the last value. "Same" keeps the last value. Skip goes to the next part and saves nothing for this one.
  • Each step saves at once. If you close the app in the middle, the values you entered are kept. Nothing is kept in the browser.
  • Lengths come only on your first log, because they do not change. Later you change them on the Measurements page.
  • The date is today. On the first step you can tap the date to log an old measurement (for example your old list).
  • "How to measure" on each step opens the full guide for that part. The picture and one line of where to measure are always on the screen.
  • Done screen. It shows all values of today with the change. Tap a value to correct it.

How it is built

PartWhat
DataA new table body_measurement · user, body part, side (left, right or none), value in cm, date. One row per value. It cascades when the account is deleted. A migration file in drizzle/.
Server ActionsLog a set of values for a date, delete one value, read all values. Each one checks the session and filters by the user. Values from 1 to 300 cm.
ScreensA new href row in Body and health (sections.tsx), a new page /more/account/measurements, and a page per body part. Reuse BackLink, the account row list, the bottom sheet and the weight chart style.
Body map and guideThe body map reuses the front figure of MuscleMap (src/lib/exercise-art/muscle-map.ts) and draws the tape lines over it. The guide text is one list in the code, with a number, the picture area and the steps for each part.
PrivacyBody measurements are health data. Add them to the data inventory, the privacy policy and the data export (collectUserData).
TestsIntegration tests for the actions (owner check, same-date replace, delete). One e2e spec that logs, reads back and deletes a value. Screenshots at 390 and 320 px.

Your decision

Decision

Body measurements are a new kind of health data. The privacy rule of this app says: name it in the policy, and ask every user to accept the consent again one time (raise CONSENT_VERSION). Each user then sees the consent page once on the next visit.

My recommendation · raise it. The alternative is a policy update notice only, but then the consent that users gave does not name this data.

What does not change

  • Height and body weight stay as they are, with the same rows and the same weight history.
  • No home widget, no chart on the Progress page, no Garmin sync of measurements.
  • The AI coach (MCP tools) does not read or write measurements in this change. That can be a later change.
  • Friends never see measurements.

How you check the result

  1. Open Account › Body and health. The row Measurements is there and says "Not set".
  2. Log your old list with an old date. Then log new values with today's date.
  3. The page shows the latest values and the change. Tap Waist and see two points in the chart.
  4. Delete one value. It goes from the history.
  5. Download your data in Your data. The measurements are in the file.
