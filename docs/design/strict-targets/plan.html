Strict session targets

Each bike and run step tells you the exact point to hit: power or pace, heart rate and cadence. The AI coach works out your numbers from your Garmin data and proposes them. You accept them before they apply.

Decisions made

New numbersThe coach proposes. You accept or decline. Nothing changes without your yes.
How strictEach work step has a full target for every place you can do it: inside and outside. What it needs depends on what you can measure there (table below). Warm-up, cool-down and recovery need one target.
By what you can measureInside, the trainer and the belt give exact numbers. Outside, heart rate, time and distance lead. The app has no live GPS; Garmin records speed and pace.
Shown in the appAll targets show on the ride screen, the run screen and the step list before you start.

Targets by place

A work step must have every target in the "Required" column for both places. The app shows the target for the place you pick at the start.

What does not change

  • FTP and run test rides keep their own targets. Ramps and free spins are exempt.
  • "Sync from Garmin" on Account keeps working as now. When it is on, Garmin values still apply without asking. The coach proposals are separate.
  • The app does not connect to a sensor. You see the targets during the session, and Garmin gives the measured values after it.
  • Swim steps do not change.

Steps in order

  1. Show all targets. Ride screen indoors and outdoors, run screen, both step lists. Run cadence in spm. Also the chart fix: "120" and "135 min" overlap at the end of the time axis.
  2. Strict work steps. The new check for bike and run work steps, in MCP and in the step editor. The editor gets a heart rate field next to power or pace.
  3. Coach proposals. A store for proposals, an MCP tool for the coach to send one, Garmin cadence for the coach to read, and the Accept and Decline card. Privacy policy and record of processing updated.
  4. Your programs. The coach reads your data, proposes your numbers, and after your yes rewrites the steps of your bike and run programs. This is a coach task, not code.

Each step is its own PR, with tests and a screenshot next to this picture. No deploy while you train.

How you check

  • Open the Long ride. The step list shows HR and cadence. Start it: the target card shows both.
  • Open a run program. The list and the run screen show HR and cadence.
  • In the step editor, remove the cadence from a bike work step. Save says what is missing.
  • Ask the coach for new numbers. A proposal shows in Account › Thresholds. Accept it, and the value is in the threshold history with the source "coach".

Open questions

Store your typical cadence (bike and run) as two new thresholds, next to FTP and LTHR?

My pick · yes. The coach then sets each step's range from it, for example 85-95 rpm in Z2 and 90-100 in intervals.

Your watch counts run cadence outside too (from the wrist). Show a cadence target on outside runs as well?

My pick · show it on the second line, but do not require it. Inside it is required.

The coach proposes your treadmill speed per zone (for example Z2 at 9.5 km/h) from the belt speeds you logged and your heart rate on them. Use the belt speeds the app logged, not the watch's indoor speed?

My pick · yes. The watch guesses speed inside from your wrist; the belt is exact.

Show a pending proposal on the home screen too, or only in Account › Thresholds?

My pick · only in Account › Thresholds, with a dot on the Account row.
