Bike ride logger

Plan for a full overhaul of the bike exercise inside a program. It plans, runs and logs a ride like a pro cycling logger. Nothing new sits beside it: the ride lives in the bike exercise you already have.

What the reviews changed

  • Nothing to type while you ride. Each step saves its time on its own. One results table comes after the ride, and Garmin fills it.
  • A clear ride life. A ride is running, paused, finished or ended early. The server keeps one list of what happened (start, pause, skip, +1 min, ±5 W). The clock, the saved steps and the pushes all come from that list, so a locked phone, a second phone or a reload agree.
  • Safer controls. Hold to skip, undo after each change, Lap only where it does something, End ride when paused, bigger buttons.
  • Right cycling numbers. 135-145 W is upper zone 2 for your FTP of 180 W, not tempo. The heart rate in your note (158-163) is zone 3 to 4 and does not match those watts; the AI fixes it. NP and IF come only from Garmin's second-by-second data, never from step averages. The mockup's totals are now correct.
  • Outdoor targets from the sport. Outdoors the main target is heart rate, made from the power zone, or effort on short hard steps. Speed is a result, never a target. Gradient is a note ("find a 4-6% climb"), not a target.
  • Mode guess. Indoor or Outdoor comes from the planned day, then from your last ride of this program, then from the month. After the ride, Garmin's ride type corrects it.
  • Groundwork first. A new step 0 adds the data fields and the ride state, so no step needs a later one.
  • Garmin fill by time overlap. It matches the Garmin ride that overlaps your ride, splits it by the real step times, and asks if two rides match. Numbers you typed are kept. Undo restores them and the same ride does not fill again.

Indoor and outdoor

Every ride has both modes. The row under the title shows the mode, and Change switches it before the start. The app picks the mode from the planned day first, then your last ride of this program, then the month (November to March Indoor). After the ride, Garmin's ride type corrects it. The mode is fixed when the ride starts.

The AI writes the indoor target. The app makes the outdoor target from it with a fixed zone table (power zone to heart rate zone), and the AI can write its own outdoor target to override. A step with no outdoor target is never refused. An outdoor target changes only the target, never how the step ends, so both modes have the same steps.

Ride rules

Life of a ride

  • States: ready, running, paused, finished, ended early.
  • One active ride per person. A second phone follows it and can take over.
  • The server keeps a list of events: start, pause, resume, skip, +1 min, ±5 W, lap, end. The clock, the saved steps and the pushes come from it. Server time is the clock.
  • Finished: the last step ends, or you tap End ride. Ended early keeps the done steps, counts load from them only, and does not mark a key session as done.
  • When the app opens on a ride that is still running, it offers Resume or End at the last step. A ride still running 2 hours after its planned end ends on its own.
  • Log by hand is hidden while a ride runs.

Changes during a ride

  • ±5 W and +1 min change this ride only, never the program. Each step saves its change next to the plan, and the results compare with the plan.
  • ±5 W moves both ends of a range or a ramp, for the current step only, at most ±50 W. Outdoors on a heart rate step there is no ±5 W.
  • Recovery in a repeat can take +1 min. The recovery after the last repeat is dropped.
  • An AI change reaches only steps that have not started, when the app is open or comes back to the front. Steps already saved keep their copy of the plan.
  • Watts are worked out from your FTP when the step starts and saved with it. A later FTP change does not rewrite history.

Numbers and load

  • NP, IF and load from power come only from Garmin's second-by-second data.
  • Without it: avg power, and load from step averages when power covers 90% of the ride and an FTP is set, marked as an estimate. Otherwise load from effort. Nothing is guessed.
  • Planned load from the step targets (about 55 for this ride).
  • Each saved number keeps where it came from: typed, the clock or Garmin.

Garmin fill

  • Match: a Garmin bike ride whose time overlaps the app ride, within 5 minutes. Two matches: the app asks.
  • Split: by Garmin laps when they line up with the steps, else by the step times from the event list, measured from Garmin's start and its pauses.
  • Typed numbers stay. Undo puts back what was there and marks that Garmin ride as declined, so a re-sync does not fill it again.
  • A Garmin ride that comes days later fills only empty fields.

What does not change

  • Strength exercises, sets, timers and logging.
  • Programs, the Plan tab, cycles, seasons and dated sessions. A planned ride still counts as done on the week page.
  • A zone target still moves with the phase of your cycle.
  • Run and swim keep today's steps and player. They get the new parts later.
  • No new tab or page type.

Limits of a web app on an iPhone

What works

  • Push with the screen off or in another app, when the app is on the home screen (iOS 16.4 and later).
  • Screen kept on and sound cues while the app is open, with a beep in the last 3 seconds of a step.
  • Garmin numbers after the ride.

What does not

  • No live watts or heart rate: Safari on iPhone cannot use Bluetooth. You read them from the bike computer, or Garmin fills them after.
  • No smart trainer control (ERG mode). A Garmin Edge or watch can do this; see decision 2.
  • A push can come a few seconds late. A pause made offline does not move the pushes.
