Session check

After each bike or run session you see which Garmin activity the app matched it to, and each target against what you did: power, heart rate, cadence and time. A home widget shows the last session; a page shows every step.

Why

Your Long ride today was matched and filled from Garmin, but no screen says so. The plan said 110-130 W and you rode 150 W on average. The app showed no flag, because it marks only "under target". It never checks heart rate zones on the bike or cadence. And after 12 hours the ride summary is gone from the app.

How it judges

  • In · the step's average is inside the range.
  • Over or under · outside the range by more than a small margin (3 W, 2 bpm, 2 rpm or spm, 5 s per km).
  • A cap ("HR under 162") passes when the average is under it.
  • Heart rate is not judged on steps under 3 min. It rises too slowly.
  • Time · in when within 10 % of the plan, as the Plan colours do today.
  • Pace outside is shown, not judged. Outside the heart rate leads.

4 · Which sessions it covers

  • Started in the app, filled from Garmin (like today) · each step's numbers come from the fill.
  • Done only on the watch · the app already matches it to the planned session by sport and day. New · the steps are split by Garmin laps when the counts match, else by the planned step times.
  • Started in the app, no Garmin · the steps show what the app logged (time, belt, your taps).

What does not change

  • The matching rules and the Plan colours stay as they are.
  • Garmin parts show only for accounts with Garmin (today Premium).
  • The ride and run summaries after a session keep their place. They use the same judging, so they also show "over".

Steps in order

  1. One judging rule for power, heart rate, cadence, pace and time, with in, over and under. The ride and run summaries use it.
  2. The match · keep which Garmin activity a session was matched or filled from, show it on the planned session and the program's exercise page, with Pick when nothing is matched.
  3. The step page · for any done bike or run session, from Plan and History.
  4. Watch-only sessions · split by laps or planned times.
  5. The home widget · last done bike or run session, on by default for endurance users.
